ABOUT LUXE AURA HAIR

Book NowCall Now

Luxe Aura Hair is a true full-service salon located in Greenwich Village, one of New York City's most beloved and characterful neighbourhoods. Built from the ground up around hair extensions, Luxe Aura Hair has become a destination for clients across the city and beyond, not because of convenience alone, but because word travels when a salon consistently delivers results that look and feel completely natural.

Luxe Aura Hair offers six professional hair extension methods: Microlink extensions, K-Tip keratin bond extensions, Tape-In extensions, Natural Beaded Rows, Brazilian Knots, and traditional sew-in weaves, all using 100% human Slavic hair sourced from Central, Eastern, and Southeastern Europe. With over 80 hair extension colours in stock, we offer a wide selection to help you find your ideal match. Same-day hair extension installations may be available on days our salon is open, subject to appointment availability. Contact us to schedule a consultation and confirm same-day installation options.

Beyond extensions, our full-service menu includes Balayage and highlights, full and single process colour, Keratin treatments, Brazilian Blowouts, Olaplex treatments, toner, relaxers, and grey coverage, everything you need for your hair's colour, condition, and care, all in one place.

Luxe Aura Hair specialises in serving clients that many other salons are simply not prepared for, those recovering from chemotherapy or dealing with alopecia, clients with fine or thinning hair, short hair, damaged hair, men seeking non-surgical hair replacement, transgender and non-binary clients, and clients with natural or textured hair. These are not niche add-ons. They are some of the most meaningful works we do, and every one of these clients receives the same level of expertise and attentiveness as anyone else.

frequently asked questions

Do you offer same-day hair extension installations?

Yes, same-day hair extension installations may be available on days our salon is open, subject to appointment availability. Luxe Aura Hair carries over 80 hair extension colours in stock, allowing clients to explore different shades and extension methods without necessarily needing to pre-order hair. You can schedule a consultation to discuss your preferred method, select a suitable colour, and check whether installation can be completed during the same visit. To confirm same-day availability, call us at (212) 221-3881 or book online at https://luxeaurahair.com/book-now.

Can you help clients with hair loss, alopecia, or post-chemo regrowth?

Absolutely, and it is one of the areas Luxe Aura Hair is most experienced and most passionate about. The team specialises in working with clients who are recovering from chemotherapy, managing alopecia, or dealing with significant hair thinning or loss. We work with as little as three to four inches of regrowth and use the gentlest, most appropriate methods for each client's specific stage and hair condition. Every appointment of this kind begins with a free, confidential consultation so we can assess your hair properly and create a plan that works for you.

How do I get started at Luxe Aura Hair?

The best first step is booking a consultation, either in person at our Greenwich Village salon or by phone. During the consultation, one of our specialists will assess your hair, talk through your goals, explain which methods are best suited to your hair type, and give you a full, transparent quote with no surprises. There is zero obligation to book after the consultation, but most clients do, because once you understand exactly what is possible, it is hard not to be excited. You can book at luxeaurahair.com/book-now or call us at (212) 221-3881. We look forward to meeting you.
