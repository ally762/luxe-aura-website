how do fusion extensions work?

Individual strands of extensions are attached to your own hair using a keratin protein bond. keratin is a natural protein found in human hair follicles. this protein aids in the growth and health of our hair. this makes the keratin bond very healthy for the hair.

what are pros & cons of fusion extensions?

Many people ask how long do fusion extensions last? one of the pros to choosing the fusion extensions is that the method can be worn for 3-5 months consecutively without the need for professional adjustments like most other kinds of hair extensions. just like the microlink extensions, you can easily run your fingers through the scalp of your head while wearing the fusion extensions. while there are very few cons to choosing the fusion extensions nyc a major factor to consider is this type of extensions might not be a good fit for candidates with curly or kinky textured hair mainly due to the fact that it is difficult to care for curly new growth which might become severely knotted over the lengthy period.

will fusion extensions damage my hair?

No, when applied by our certified experts, fusion extensions are safe for your hair. Each strand is attached with a keratin protein bond, a natural protein found in human hair that aids in its growth and health. Special care must be taken with heavy conditioners, oils, and heating tools. Fusion extensions may not be a good fit for curly or kinky textured hair or for clients experiencing rapid hair loss, so we recommend a consultation first.
