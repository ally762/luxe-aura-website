Frequently Asked Questions

What hair extension methods does Luxe Aura Hair offer and how do I know which one is right for me?

We offer six professional hair extension methods, Microlink extensions, K-Tip keratin bond extensions, Tape-In extensions, Natural Beaded Rows, Brazilian Knots, and traditional sew-in weaves. Each method is suited to different hair types, lifestyles, and goals. Microlinks are ideal for fine or fragile hair because they involve no heat or adhesive. K-Tip extensions work beautifully for almost any hair type and deliver the most natural individual movement. Tape-ins offer the fastest application and a completely flat, seamless finish. Natural Beaded Rows are the go-to for maximum volume and fullness. Brazilian Knots are exceptionally durable and work across all textures including curly and natural hair. The best way to know which method is right for you specifically is to come in for a free consultation, our specialists assess your hair in person and give you a clear, honest recommendation based on your hair's density, condition, and the result you want to achieve.

What makes Luxe Aura Hair different from other extension salons in NYC?

Every extension we install uses 100% human Slavic hair, ethically sourced from Central, Eastern, and Southeastern Europe, widely considered the finest hair in the world for extension purposes. Slavic hair is naturally fine in strand diameter, which allows it to blend seamlessly with most hair types, and its cuticle is fully intact and aligned in a single direction, which means it does not tangle, does not lose its shine rapidly, and maintains its quality across multiple application cycles. We carry over 80 colours in stock at all times, which means there is never a wait for your hair to be ordered and same-day installations are available every single day. The combination of premium sourcing, expert application, and genuine colour-matching expertise is what separates a Luxe Aura Hair result from what most salons in the city can deliver.

Do you offer hair extensions for people with hair loss, thinning hair, or those recovering from chemotherapy?

Yes, and it is one of the most important parts of what we do. We specialise in hair extensions for clients experiencing hair loss from a wide range of causes including chemotherapy, alopecia, postpartum shedding, hormonal thinning, and damage from years of chemical or heat styling. For post-chemo clients, we regularly work with as little as three to four inches of regrowth and use the gentlest available methods to restore length, volume, and confidence. For clients with fine or thinning hair, we select lightweight, low-tension methods that add density without stressing fragile strands. Every hair loss consultation at Luxe Aura Hair is free, private, completely unhurried, and approached with the sensitivity and expertise these appointments deserve.

Is Luxe Aura Hair welcoming to transgender and non-binary clients?

Completely and without qualification. Our team includes members of the LGBT community, and our welcome to transgender, non-binary, gender non-conforming, and crossdressing clients is genuine and built on real experience rather than surface-level inclusivity. We understand the specific hair challenges that MTF transgender women often face — including male hairline patterns, hormonal changes affecting hair density and texture, and the need for a result that is both natural-looking and versatile enough for different environments. Our semi-private studio means your appointment is private and unhurried, you will be referred to correctly throughout, and every member of our team approaches these appointments with the care and professionalism they deserve. Many of our transgender clients found us through word of mouth within the community, which is the most honest endorsement we could ask for.

Do you offer hair services beyond extensions, and can I get everything done in one visit?

Yes, Luxe Aura Hair is a full-service salon. Beyond our six extension methods, we offer a complete professional colour and treatment menu including Balayage, full and partial highlights, full head colour, single process colour, Keratin treatments, Brazilian Blowouts, Olaplex treatments, toner, relaxers, and grey coverage. Many clients combine services in a single visit, for example, having extensions installed and a Balayage or toning service done in the same appointment to create a completely unified, dimensional colour result from root to tip. Same-day service is available every day, so if your schedule demands efficiency, we are built for it.

Do you offer hair replacement services for men, and how does the non-surgical system work?

Yes. We offer non-surgical hair replacement systems specifically designed for men experiencing thinning or hair loss at any stage, from mild thinning to more significant loss. The system is a lightweight unit made from 100% real virgin human hair attached to a skin-like base that mimics the appearance of your natural scalp. It is placed directly over the hair loss area, custom-fitted to your head shape, and styled to blend seamlessly with your existing hair. The result is completely undetectable by sight or touch, can be washed and styled exactly like your own hair, and requires a simple retightening appointment every six to eight weeks. There is no surgery, no recovery period, and no permanent commitment. Same-day consultations are available, and the system can be ready either the same day or within one week depending on your specific requirements.
