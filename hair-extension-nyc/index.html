Manhattan's Most Trusted Hair Extension Specialists, Greenwich Village, New York City

Hair extensions are one of the most transformative services in modern beauty — and when done right, they are completely undetectable, incredibly natural-feeling, and life-changing. At Luxe Aura Hair Salon in the heart of Greenwich Village, Manhattan, we have built our entire practice around doing them right. Every extension we install is crafted from 100% human Slavic hair, ethically sourced across Central, Eastern, and Southeastern Europe, a region renowned for producing some of the finest, most versatile hair in the world. This hair is prized for its silky texture, natural strength, and its remarkable ability to blend seamlessly with most hair types, whether your natural hair is fine and straight, thick and wavy, or naturally textured and curly.

We offer six distinct application methods and over 80 colour variations, which means no two clients leave looking the same, and every result is entirely custom to you. Our team of expert stylists takes the time to understand your hair, your lifestyle, your goals, and your concerns before recommending any method. Whether you are coming to us for the first time, recovering from hair loss, navigating a personal transformation, or simply wanting more length and volume, we treat every consultation with the same level of care and expertise. Same-day service is available.

01

Every Method We Offer — And How to Choose the Right One

Choosing the right hair extension method is the most important decision in your extension journey, and it is one our stylists help you make with confidence. K-Tip extensions, also called keratin bond extensions, are among the most popular methods we offer. Individual strands are fused to your natural hair using a low-heat keratin protein bond, creating extensions that move, feel, and behave exactly like your own hair. They last three to six months and are reusable, making them an excellent long-term investment for almost any hair type.

Microlink extensions, sometimes called microbead extensions, use tiny silicone-lined rings instead of heat or adhesive, making them one of the gentlest methods available. This makes them ideal for fine, fragile, or previously damaged hair. Tape-in extensions use ultra-thin wefts with medical-grade adhesive that lie completely flat to the scalp, offering one of the fastest application times and a beautifully seamless finish that works especially well for adding volume quickly. Invisible beaded weft extensions, or hand-tied extensions, are sewn onto small beaded rows and distribute weight evenly across the scalp, the go-to method for clients who want maximum fullness and natural movement. Brazilian knot extensions use a specialised thread-knotting technique that works beautifully across all textures, including curly and natural hair types, offering exceptional durability and flexibility. And for clients who need full coverage, our lace front wigs crafted from 100% human Slavic hair provide a completely natural-looking hairline with the freedom to change your look entirely whenever you choose.

No single method is universally the best; the right one depends on your natural hair density, your lifestyle, how much maintenance you are comfortable with, and what result you are hoping to achieve. That is exactly why we offer every consultation at no charge.

Specialised Extensions for Every Hair Story

Hair loss, thin hair, short hair, medical recovery, and gender transition are not obstacles to getting beautiful extensions. At Luxe Aura Hair, they are exactly the kind of challenges our team is trained and passionate about addressing. For clients who have completed chemotherapy treatment, we specialise in working with as little as three to four inches of regrowth, using the gentlest possible methods to restore length, volume, and most importantly, confidence. We understand that this stage of recovery is deeply personal, and we approach every appointment with the sensitivity and patience it deserves.

For clients with fine or thinning hair, we focus on lightweight methods that add density without placing stress on fragile strands. For short hair, even pixie cuts and bobs, our blending expertise means the transition from your natural length to extension length is completely invisible. We also offer non-surgical hair replacement systems specifically designed for men experiencing thinning or hair loss, crafted to match your exact colour and texture for results that are completely undetectable in everyday life. Our salon is a fully welcoming and affirming space for transgender and non-binary clients, where every consultation is confidential, compassionate, and tailored entirely to your transition goals. For Black women and clients with natural or textured hair, we apply methods that honour your curl pattern and hair's natural integrity while delivering the dramatic length or volume transformation you are looking for. Whatever your hair story, there is a solution at Luxe Aura Hair.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do hair extensions last, and how often do they need maintenance?

The lifespan of your extensions depends on the method and how well they are cared for at home. K-Tip and Brazilian Knot extensions typically last three to six months before a reapplication appointment is needed. Tape-in and beaded weft extensions require a maintenance visit every six to eight weeks as your natural hair grows. Microlink extensions are generally refreshed every two to four months. The good news is that with all of our methods, the Slavic human hair itself is reusable, so you are not repurchasing hair at every appointment, only paying for the stylist's time and any supplemental strands needed.

Will hair extensions damage my natural hair?

When installed and maintained correctly by a trained professional, hair extensions should not damage your natural hair. The key is choosing the right method for your hair type, particularly if your hair is fine, fragile, or previously chemically treated. At Luxe Aura Hair, we assess your hair's density and condition before recommending any method. Methods like Microlink and Beaded Weft are especially gentle because they involve no heat and no adhesive. We also provide detailed aftercare guidance after every installation to help you protect your natural hair throughout the wear period.

Can I colour, heat-style, and wash my extensions like normal hair?

Yes, because our extensions are made from 100% human Slavic hair, they respond to heat styling, washing, and colouring just like your natural hair does. You can blow-dry, straighten, and curl them freely. We do recommend using sulphate-free shampoos and conditioning treatments to maintain the hair's quality and extend its life. If you are considering colouring your extensions, we always recommend having that done in the salon rather than at home to ensure the colour integrates seamlessly with your natural hair.

I have very short or very thin hair — am I still a candidate for extensions?

Absolutely. Short and thin hair are two of the most common starting points we work with, and they are entirely manageable with the right method. For short hair, K-Tip and Brazilian Knots are typically the strongest fit because they can be attached at shorter lengths and blended expertly. For fine or thinning hair, Microlink extensions are often our first recommendation because of their lightweight, adhesive-free application. We have also helped many clients in post-chemotherapy recovery stages with as little as three to four inches of regrowth, so please do not let your current hair length or density hold you back from booking a consultation.

How much do hair extensions cost at Luxe Aura Hair?

The cost of hair extensions varies depending on the method you choose, the amount of hair needed to achieve your desired result, and the length and colour of the Slavic hair selected. Because every client's hair and goals are different, we do not publish a one-size-fits-all price , instead, we offer a completely free, no-obligation consultation where your stylist will give you a precise quote based on exactly what your transformation requires. There are no surprises and no pressure. To get started, simply book your free consultation online or call us directly at (212) 221-3881.
