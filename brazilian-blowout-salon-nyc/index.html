frequently asked questions

What is a Brazilian Blowout?

A Brazilian Blowout is a smoothing treatment that reduces frizz, adds shine, and makes hair more manageable using a protective protein layer.

What is the difference between Brazilian Blowout and keratin treatment?

Brazilian Blowout smooths hair and reduces frizz without fully removing curls, while keratin treatments soften curls more and require 24–72 hours before washing.

How long does a Brazilian Blowout last?

Results typically last 4 to 6 months, depending on hair type, growth, and maintenance.

Can I wash my hair after a Brazilian Blowout?

Yes, one major benefit is that you can wash your hair immediately after the treatment.

Is Brazilian Blowout suitable for all hair types?

Yes, it works best on thick, curly, frizzy, or kinky hair, but most hair types can benefit from it.

How long does a Brazilian Blowout appointment take?

The process usually takes about 90 minutes or more, depending on hair length and thickness.
